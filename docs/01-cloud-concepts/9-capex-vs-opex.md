# CapEx vs OpEx

## Definition
Capital expenditure (CapEx) and operational expenditure (OpEx) describe different ways of paying for technology resources.

## CapEx
CapEx is an upfront investment in assets that an organization purchases and owns.

Examples include buying servers, networking equipment, and datacenter hardware.

## OpEx
OpEx is ongoing spending for services or resources as they are consumed.

Cloud consumption commonly aligns with an OpEx model because usage can be paid for over time rather than requiring all infrastructure to be purchased upfront.

## Comparison
| Factor | CapEx | OpEx |
|---|---|---|
| Spending | Upfront | Ongoing / consumption-based |
| Typical example | Purchase servers | Pay for cloud usage |
| Asset ownership | Organization owns assets | Consume provider service |

## Decision Factors
```text
BUY infrastructure upfront
→ CapEx

CONSUME / pay as used
→ OpEx
```

## Exam Reasoning
Classify the **financial model**, not the technology itself. A scenario describing upfront equipment purchases points to CapEx; consumption-based cloud spending points to OpEx.
