# Elasticity

## Definition
Elasticity is the ability to dynamically increase or decrease resources as demand changes.

## What Problem Does It Solve?
Workloads may have temporary peaks and drops. Elasticity allows capacity to follow demand instead of permanently provisioning for the highest possible load.

## Decision Factors
```text
Demand rises
→ capacity increases

Demand falls
→ capacity decreases

Dynamic response to demand
→ Elasticity
```

## Elasticity vs Scalability
| Concept | Focus |
|---|---|
| **Scalability** | Ability to change capacity |
| **Elasticity** | Dynamic adaptation of capacity to demand |

## Exam Reasoning
If the scenario emphasizes resources automatically or dynamically expanding and contracting with demand, think **elasticity**.
