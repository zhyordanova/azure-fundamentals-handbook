# Geo-distribution

## Definition
Geo-distribution places applications, services, or data across multiple geographic locations.

## What Problem Does It Solve?
Geographic distribution can help serve users closer to their location and can support resilient multi-region designs.

## Common Benefits
- reduced latency for geographically distributed users
- geographic resiliency
- support for disaster-recovery designs

## Geo-distribution vs High Availability
```text
Distribute geographically
→ Geo-distribution

Keep service available despite failures
→ High Availability
```

A geo-distributed design can improve availability, but geo-distribution describes **where** workloads or data are placed.

## Exam Reasoning
Look for a geographic requirement such as users in multiple parts of the world, latency across locations, or deployment across regions.
