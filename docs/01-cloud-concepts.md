# 1. What is Cloud Computing?

Cloud computing is the delivery of computing services—including compute, storage, networking, databases, and software—over the Internet.

Instead of purchasing and maintaining physical hardware, organizations consume IT resources on demand and pay only for what they use.

## Why does Cloud exist?

Traditional IT requires:

- Purchasing hardware
- Installing servers
- Maintaining datacenters
- Replacing hardware
- Planning capacity years in advance

Cloud computing removes most of this operational burden.
